# Task 3 – Data Visualization & Dashboarding

## Dataset

## Objective

## Key Insights
- Month-to-month customers show the highest churn.
- Customers with Fiber Optic service churn more frequently.
- Higher monthly charges are associated with increased churn.
- Long-term customers are less likely to churn.
- Electronic check is a common payment method among churned customers.
